✈️ Dubrovnik Airport — LDDU

Aircraft can be seen seconds before touchdown on RWY 11. Excellent position for close approach photographs.

🔵 Spot 2

Very good view of aircraft on the taxiway and the General Aviation apron.

🔴 Spot 4 — HNK Konavljanin

Particularly useful when RWY 29 is in use.

Elevated position overlooking Dubrovnik Airport and the surrounding area.
