✈️ Croatia Planespotting

LDDU / DBV • Dubrovnik Ruđer Bošković Airport

📍 Planespotting mjesta

✈️ Dubrovnik Airport — LDDU

🟢 Spot 1 — RWY 11 Approach

Aircraft can be seen seconds before touchdown on RWY 11. Excellent position for close approach photographs.

🔵 Spot 2 — Aircraft Throughout Their Movement

Very good all-around position where you can follow aircraft for a large part of their movement around the airport.

🟠 Spot 3 — Taxiway / General Aviation Apron

Climb onto the wall for a great view of aircraft on the taxiway and the entire General Aviation apron. Aircraft vacating via E can also be photographed very well.

🔴 Spot 4 — HNK Konavljanin

Very good location when RWY 29 is in use. Particularly useful for arrivals and departures from this direction.

🟣 Spot 5 — Hill / Airport Panorama

Elevated position with aircraft in the distance and a panoramic view of the entire airport and Dubrovnik Old Town.

✈️ Split Airport — LDSP

🟢 Spot 1 — RWY 23 Arrival

Excellent position for aircraft arriving on RWY 23. Great for photographing aircraft on final approach.

🔵 Spot 2 — RWY 23 End

Very good position near the end of RWY 23 with excellent views of arriving and departing aircraft.

🟠 Spot 3 — RWY 23 Touchdown

Great location for touchdown shots and aircraft on short final when RWY 23 is in use.

🔴 Spot 4 — RWY 05

Good position for aircraft operating around the RWY 05 end, especially when RWY 05 is active.

🟣 Spot 5 — RWY 05 Line-up

Good view of aircraft lining up, backtracking and operating on RWY 05.

🔷 Spot 6 — Observation Deck

Observation position at the terminal with views of aircraft movements around Split Airport.

🟣 Spot 7 — Terminal

Position around the terminal area with views of aircraft movements and airport operations.

🩷 Spot 8 — Mountain Spot

Elevated position west of the airport with a wide view of the airport. Good for RWY 05 arrivals and RWY 23 departures.
