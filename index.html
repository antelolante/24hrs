📍 Planespotting mjesta

✈️ Dubrovnik Airport — LDDU

🟢 Spot 1 — RWY 11 Approach

Aircraft can be seen seconds before touchdown on RWY 11. Excellent position for close approach photographs.

🔵 Spot 2 — Airport Area

Very good all-around spotting position with views of aircraft operating around Dubrovnik Airport.

🟠 Spot 3 — Taxiway / General Aviation Apron

Very good view of aircraft on the taxiway and the General Aviation apron. Excellent for taxiing and vacating shots.

🔴 Spot 4 — HNK Konavljanin

Very good position when RWY 29 is in use.

🟣 Spot 5 — Hill Position

Hill position overlooking Dubrovnik Airport, providing a wide view of the airport and surrounding area.

✈️ Split Airport — LDSP

🟢 Spot 1 — RWY 23 Arrival

Excellent for aircraft arriving on RWY 23.

🔵 Spot 2 — RWY 23 End

Very good for aircraft movements around the end of RWY 23.

🟠 Spot 3 — RWY 23 Short Final / Touchdown

Excellent position for touchdown photographs.

🔴 Spot 4 — RWY 05 Turning Area

Useful for aircraft operating around the RWY 05 turning area.

🟣 Spot 5 — RWY 05 Line-up / Approach

Good position for RWY 05 line-ups and approaching aircraft.

🔷 Spot 6 — Observation Deck

Observation position with views toward airport operations.

🩷 Spot 7 — Terminal / Observation Position

Useful position around the terminal and observation area.

🟡 Spot 8 — Elevated Position

Elevated position west of the airport. Good for RWY 05 arrivals and RWY 23 departures.

✈️ Zadar Airport — LDZD

🟢 Spot 1 — Fence Front

Position for 13/31 movements. Best for RWY 31 arrivals and line-ups.

🔵 Spot 2 — Terminal Fence

Apron position suitable for parked aircraft and apron movements.

🟠 Spot 3 — RWY 22 Threshold

Good for RWY 04/22 movements, including line-ups and aircraft vacating the runway.

🔴 Spot 4 — Gate P7

Useful for RWY 04 arrivals and line-ups.

🟣 Spot 5 — RWY 04 Head

Good for RWY 04 arrivals and line-ups.

🔷 Spot 6 — Highway Turnoff

Position primarily useful for RWY 04 arrivals.

✈️ Rijeka Airport — LDRI

🟢 Spot 1 — Terminal & Rental Car Apron View

Good for regional traffic and airliners parked on the ramp or taxiing toward the runway.

🔵 Spot 2 — RWY 14 Touchdown

North perimeter position for low-altitude RWY 14 landing shots.

🟠 Spot 3 — RWY 32 Touchdown

South touchdown position for arrivals from the southeast.

🔴 Spot 4 — General Aviation Apron

Good position for private jets and small aircraft.

✈️ Zagreb Franjo Tuđman Airport — LDZA

🟢 Spot 1 — Runway / Taxiway View

Public roadside spotting position beside the airport. Good views of aircraft operating around the runway and taxiway.

🔵 Spot 2 — RWY 23 Approach

Northern approach area with views toward aircraft approaching RWY 23.

🟠 Spot 3 — Terminal / Cargo Area

Position with views toward the terminal, apron, cargo and maintenance traffic.

⚠️ Photography information

Photography must be carried out only from areas where photography is permitted. Do not enter restricted airport operational areas, runways, taxiways or aircraft stands. Follow all instructions given by airport personnel and current airport rules.
